📜 Monopoly 3D Official Rules & Tutorial

🎯 Game Objective & Overview

The object of Monopoly 3D is to become the wealthiest player through buying, renting, and trading real estate properties, ultimately forcing all opponents into bankruptcy.

1. Roll & Move

Click Roll Dice on your turn. Your token moves clockwise according to the total of both dice.

2. Buy or Auction

If you land on unowned property, you may purchase it from the Bank at face value, or send it to public auction for open bidding.

3. Pay or Collect Rent

Landing on another player's property requires paying them rent. Unimproved monopoly sets charge double rent; houses and hotels charge massive sums!

4. Rolling Doubles

If both dice show the same number, you take an immediate bonus roll! But be careful: rolling doubles 3 times in one turn sends you straight to Jail.

🏠 Properties, Monopolies & Hotels

The board features 28 properties: 22 colored street properties divided into 8 color groups, 4 Railroads, and 2 Utilities.

⭐ Color Monopolies (2x Rent Multiplier)

When a player owns all properties of a single color group (e.g. both Dark Blue properties, or all three Red properties), the base rent for unimproved properties in that color group is doubled!

🏗️ Building Houses & Grand Hotels

Once you possess a monopoly, you can purchase Houses evenly across your group. Each house dramatically multiplies the rent. When you have 4 houses on a property, you can upgrade to a Grand Hotel to bankrupt careless travelers!

🚂 Railroads & 💡 Utilities

• Railroads: 1 RR = $25 | 2 RRs = $50 | 3 RRs = $100 | 4 RRs = $200.
• Utilities: Rent is 4x dice roll with 1 utility, or 10x dice roll if owning both Electric Company and Water Works!

🔒 Corner & Special Board Spaces

COLLECT $200 SALARY AS YOU PASS GO:

Every time your token lands on or passes GO in clockwise progression, you collect $200 from the Bank.

IN JAIL / JUST VISITING:

Landing directly on Jail means you are "Just Visiting" with no penalty.
If sent to Jail (via Go to Jail tile, 3 doubles, or Chance/Chest card), you are incarcerated. You can get out by:
1. Rolling doubles on any of your next 3 turns.
2. Paying a $50 fine before rolling.
3. Using a "Get Out of Jail Free" card.
*Important: You still collect full rent from other players while serving time in Jail!

FREE PARKING:

A safe resting spot where no rent or fines are owed.

CHANCE & COMMUNITY CHEST:

Draw a card containing surprise prizes, teleportation orders, bank dividends, or repair costs.

INCOME TAX ($200) & LUXURY TAX ($100):

Mandatory assessment fees paid immediately to the Bank.

🔨 Auctions, Trading & Debt Relief

🔨 Live Property Auctions

Whenever a player declines to buy an unowned property they landed on, it is automatically put up for public auction starting at $10. All players (including the player who declined) can place bids in increments of +$10 or +$50. The last player remaining wins the deed!

🤝 Player-to-Player Trading

Click the 🤝 Trade button at any time to propose mutual exchanges of properties, cash, or Jail cards with other players or AI bots. AI bots analyze fair market value before accepting.

💸 Mortgages & Avoiding Bankruptcy

If you owe rent or taxes and have insufficient cash, you can mortgage unimproved properties to the Bank for 50% of their printed purchase value. Mortgaged properties collect no rent until unmortgaged for 10% interest.
